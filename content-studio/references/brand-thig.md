## What it is
- **Thig = an "AI CMO" for founders and small teams.** Learns your business from your website, writes a
  marketing strategy, then runs **11 AI agents** (one channel each). **You approve every action** before it ships.
- Hero: "Meet Thig, your AI marketing team". Title tag: "Your AI Marketing Team for Strategy, Content and Growth".
- Company: **Wizcoder AI Labs**, Surat, Gujarat, India · hello@thig.ai · © 2026. (Same group as wizcoder.com.)
- No founder names, testimonials or customer stats on the site — **never invent any** (brand rule too).

**NO MOBILE APP (user, 2026-10-29):** THIG is a desktop web app only — never show a phone / iPhone / mobile UI.
Mascot must gesture through the film (wave, point, think, cheer); the v1 SVG's round "blob" hand was judged "very bad".

## How it works (the 4-step story — natural video structure)
1. **Thig studies your business** — reads your site, researches competitors, writes 3 core docs in **~10 minutes**:
   Product Information, Design Guide, Competitor Analysis. On demand: Brand Voice, Marketing Strategy, Content Strategy.
2. **Each agent takes one channel** — all read the same docs + brand voice, "so a Reddit reply and a blog article make the same argument".
3. **Drafts land in one feed** — posts, replies, articles, scripts, code fixes wait in the approval feed *with the reason they were suggested*.
4. **You approve, then it ships** — to CMS, GitHub or socials; anything unconnected can be copied.
Tagline line: **"Eleven agents, one strategy, every draft approved by you."**

## Positioning & voice
- Problem line: **"Small teams know marketing matters, but it loses to product work every week."**
- vs generic AI: "start from a blank prompt every time, so the output sounds like everyone else" → Thig keeps stored strategy + voice.
- vs hiring: expensive and slow. vs agency: "No. It is software you run yourself."
- Trust promises (use as proof beats): "Nothing posts, publishes or merges without a person saying yes." Won't buy followers/links,
  won't invent quotes or stats, shows the data source (the Search Console query / the AI answer) instead of an opaque score.
  Reddit & HN drafts are always posted by you. 2FA. Read-only GSC/GA4.
- Voice: short, declarative, plain, few superlatives, blunt about the problem, human-in-control. Brand VO in first person ("we"/"Thig").
- CTAs: **"Start free"**, URL input "Your website", "Compare all eleven agents".
