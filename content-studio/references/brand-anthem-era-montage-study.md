- Act I TOOLS (0–20 s): typed lowercase words on off-white + drifting specks ("long before we had *language*") → cave-art

- Act III THE MIND (40–74 s): real archival quote voice → word wall (telegraph plough rocket chip… repeated) with spoken
  words highlighted in white boxes → orange italic serif "tool." in a blue curtained room → MRI scans → pulsing orange dot
  "we built a mind." → fast product-in-the-world cuts (headlines, microscope-circle vignettes, chat UI typing, real posts,
  Mars rover) → wordmark letter-scramble reveal on soft clouds → creator signature.

## Techniques
1. Era ladder: "same instinct, different tool" → product = the next invention (emotional engine).
2. Mixed media per act (type / collage / cinematic b-roll / pencil sketch / archival VHS / scientific / real UI).
3. Every key noun cuts to its own picture; minimal lowercase type, ONE orange accent word (often italic serif).
4. Act title cards give documentary weight; silence before the final word; music swell under archival voice.
5. Wordmark reveal by glyph scramble.
Likely made with: Premiere/AE + AI video (Kling/Runway/Veo/Seedance) + stock + YouTube archival + AI narration.
